05 About

Hi, I'm
Doorgesh

Better known online as Zenn Prxd. I build custom audio systems, sim rigs, computer-vision hardware and high-voltage experiments, then write the code and make the content around them.

Drummer and leader of a school band, first-place winner at RGSC Science Quest 2026, and always in the middle of a new build.

I run WebLuxe, a web design studio in Mauritius, and have shipped sites for car dealers, a hotel, a tools shop, an auto-styling workshop and an NGO.
