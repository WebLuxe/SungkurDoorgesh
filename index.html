01Award project

The Ionic Leap

1st place, nationally Rajiv Gandhi Science Centre · Science Quest 2026

A high-voltage, atmospheric electrohydrodynamic thruster. It moves air with electricity alone: no propeller, no motor, no moving parts.

  1. 01

    High voltage on a thin wire

    A very thin emitter wire is charged to a high voltage. Because the wire is so thin, the electric field right at its surface becomes extremely strong.

  2. 02

    The air breaks down

    That field is strong enough to ionise the air around the wire. This is corona discharge: a faint violet glow and a steady supply of charged ions.

  3. 03

    Ions drag the air with them

    The ions race toward the oppositely charged collector. On the way they collide with neutral air molecules and push them along, creating a wind.

  4. 04

    Thrust, with no moving parts

    Air pushed one way means a force the other way. That is electrohydrodynamic thrust: silent, solid-state propulsion from electricity alone.
